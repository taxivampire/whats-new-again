# How six apps write a maintenance release

Each excerpt comes from the developer’s US App Store version history. The version numbers identify the entries as the storefront changes. [中文版](app-store-examples.md)

| App and version | Excerpt | Writing choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AllTrails 26.10.30 | “Minor bug fixes” | A short greeting, then three words for the actual work. [Version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/alltrails-hike-bike-run/id405075943) |
| Headspace 8.31.0 | “Thanks for using Headspace!” | A direct thank-you frames a plain maintenance note. The team ends with its support address. [Version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/headspace-sleep-meditation/id493145008) |
| Slack 26.10.40 | “We tweaked some things too small to notice or too difficult to explain.” | A team member could say this out loud. The sentence acknowledges the size of the update. [Version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/slack/id618783545) |
| Todoist 26.9.26 and 26.10.8 | “A few fixes and flourishes, that’s all.” / “We told all the bugs to bug off.” | Two different openings, one familiar voice. Both end by pointing readers to the same place in Settings. [Version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/todoist-to-do-list-calendar/id572688855) |
| Strava 486.0.2 | “Have fun out there!” | The sign-off speaks to someone about to go outside. The product’s everyday setting supplies the personality. [Version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strava-run-bike-walk/id426826309) |
| Duolingo 7.146.0 | “trading in his usual diet of mice for bugs this week” | The owl mascot gives the word “bugs” a second meaning that fits this particular app. [Version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/duolingo-learn-languages/id570060128) |

When a release includes a significant product change, describe what actually changed. Apple allows generic descriptions for simple bug fixes, security updates and performance improvements. [App Review Guidelines, section 2.3.12](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata)
